WHO WE ARE

Buildings are more than systems.
They are the spaces where life happens.

Controlium Building Technology works with real estate developers, government utilities, hotel operators, facility management companies and entrepreneurs to create smarter building environments.

Our portfolio centers on PIR motion detection and a broad sensor range, with lighting control and building management integration at the heart of connected spaces. It also includes home automation, guest room management, intelligent luminaires and structured cabling. We connect products and systems around each project’s needs, with comfort, operational efficiency and simple control in mind.
